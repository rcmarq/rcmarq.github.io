Jupyter Tools

Interactive Notebooks

These notebooks were designed to run directly in your browser via Binder — no installation required. Each tool opens a live Jupyter session in a new tab. The goal here is to facilitate learning geochemical/cosmochemical concepts quantitatively with the minimum barrier to entry. I'm open to any suggestions/comments!

NebCondense

Guides students through L. Grossman's first calculation of early nebular condensation. Developed as part of Ge 141 (Isotope Cosmochemistry) at Caltech.

ℹ️ Note on launch times: The first launch of a notebook may take 1–3 minutes while Binder builds the environment. Subsequent launches are faster. All computation happens on free Binder servers — no data is stored after you close the session.
