16 March 2026 Conference

Lunar and Planetary Science Conference LVII

I presented parts of my work from my Ph.D. that details our new estimate of the initial abundances of cesium-135 in the early Solar System. This revised estimate is much higher than published values (at least two orders of magnitude!). We are proposing that 135Cs comes from explosive nucleosynthesis akin to 27Al and 41Ca.
