Paleoclimate: Death Valley and the 41-kyr World

In collaboration with Dr. Elizabeth Niespolo (Princeton), I am working to secure funding to study saltpan sediments from the Confidence Hills Formation in Death Valley, CA. The goal is to test whether this section preserves a paleoclimatic response to precessional (23-kyr) forcing during the Plio-Pleistocene — a period (3.0–0.8 Ma) when 23-kyr power is conspicuously absent from the global climate record despite predictions from orbital theory.

I am also interested in using barium isotopes in deep-sea archives to track past changes in primary productivity and nutrient cycling, and their role in driving glacial-interglacial cycles.
